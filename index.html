Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services.

Do you guarantee a job or visa?

No. Employers, universities and relevant authorities make their own decisions. We cannot guarantee employment, admission or visa approval.

What documents may be required?

Requirements depend on the service and individual case. A document checklist should be provided after your requirements are reviewed.

Are there any consultancy fees?

Applicable service fees, payment terms and refund conditions should be disclosed before you make a payment.

How can I track my application?

The account dashboard is currently a demonstration. Application tracking will become available after the backend system is connected.

How will my documents be protected?

Sensitive documents should be handled through a secure backend system with restricted access. The live document upload system is not yet connected.

Can I withdraw my application?

Contact the consultancy to request withdrawal and information about applicable terms and procedures.
