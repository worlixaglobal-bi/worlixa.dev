Frequently asked questions


Do you guarantee a job or visa?

No. Employers and authorities make the final decisions. We assist with process and paperwork.

Who can see my documents?

Only you and authorized staff who need them. Documents sit in private storage and every access is logged.

What happens if I don’t finish my application?

Your draft is saved, and you’ll see a notice when that happens. You can resume later or withdraw, and you can opt out of reminders at any time.

Are there fees?

Any fee is stated on the service or job listing and in the refund policy before you pay.
